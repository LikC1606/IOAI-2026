## Reading the statuses

- `evidence_supported*` means only the stated evidence scope is supported.
- `known_deviation` records a conflict; it is not converted into a green check by the package.
- `jury_interpretation_risk` and `evidence_partially_available` require organizer/Jury judgment.
- Task 1/2 reproduction and formal-prefix evidence must not be substituted for the lost original official-final run.

[`REQUIREMENT_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.json`](REQUIREMENT_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.json).
